Your information, explained

Privacy notice

This notice explains what information RedeemHub handles when you browse the store, sign in, place an order, submit a UPI reference or partner-task completion, or contact support.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who operates RedeemHub

RedeemHub operates this digital gift-code marketplace. For privacy questions or requests, contact redeemhub67889@gmail.com.

Information we receive

  • Google sign-in: When you choose Google, Google provides basic account details such as your name, email address and profile information (which may include a profile image). RedeemHub uses these details to create and secure your account. We do not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive or other Google services.
  • Account and store activity: Your profile name, account identifier, referral code and referral activity, saved items, product reviews, notifications, and order details such as items, amounts and delivery status.
  • Partner-task claims: If you choose to submit a task for Spark review, we store your completion note and its review status so an administrator can verify the task with the partner and, if approved, credit the listed Sparks. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, payment PINs or other secrets.
  • Manual UPI checkout: If you submit payment details, we store the UPI transaction reference (UTR) you provide and associate it with your order so an administrator can compare it with the incoming payment. Your bank or UPI app handles the transfer. RedeemHub does not request your UPI PIN, bank password, card number or card security code.
  • Support: Information you include in a support request, such as its subject, message and related order. Please do not include passwords, verification codes, UPI PINs or card security codes.
  • Basic technical data: Your browser stores authentication session information needed to keep you signed in. Hosting and security services may process standard request and diagnostic information when they deliver the site.

How we use information

We use information to provide Google sign-in; operate accounts, referrals and saved items; create and manage orders; match customer-submitted UPI references against actual incoming transfers; review partner-task completion claims and issue Sparks after verification; release codes only after an administrator verifies payment; respond to support requests; protect the marketplace; and meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

Services that help run the store

Google provides the sign-in service. Supabase provides authentication, database, storage and application-backend services. GitHub Pages delivers the public website. These services process information as needed to provide their part of the service under their own terms and privacy notices. Your bank or UPI app processes your transfer under its own terms. RedeemHub does not sell your personal information.

Access, security and retention

Account access controls and database policies are used to limit access to customer records. Redeem codes are encrypted by the server before they are stored. No website or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

We keep account, order and support information while it is needed to operate the store, handle transactions or disputes, prevent misuse, and meet applicable record-keeping requirements. Some transaction records may need to be retained after an account is closed. We remove or de-identify information when it is no longer needed and retention is not required.

Your requests and choices

You can stop using Google sign-in at any time. To ask about the information associated with your account, or request correction or deletion, email us from the address associated with your RedeemHub account. We may need to verify the request and may retain information that is needed for an active transaction, dispute, security purpose or legal obligation.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the store changes. The date at the top shows when it was most recently revised. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
